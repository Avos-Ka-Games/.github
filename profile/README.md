## 🎮 Our games

### [Wings of Freedom](https://github.com/Avos-Ka-Games/Wings-of-Freedom)

A third-person action game inspired by Javanese *wayang kulit* shadow theater. You are a young Garuda searching for your mother, kidnapped by the Lord of Scales.

🏆 **1st place** at the international "CyberSmena: Moscow — Indonesia" marathon (Skolkovo) · built in **2 days** by a team of 4

`Unity 6` · `Windows` · `RU / ID`
